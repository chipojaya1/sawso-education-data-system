## The story

1. **Lost in the halls:** getting to know the people and the challenge.
2. **What I accomplished:** school-register work, KoboToolbox prototypes and clearer reporting concepts.

## Important scope note

This presentation is a project reflection and concept demonstration, **not a deployed application**. The dashboard prototype is a static HTML/CSS/JS mockup with illustrative data. It is not connected to the Master School Register, KoboToolbox or Power BI. The DRC school-to-office mapping, reporting decisions, scoring, access rules and pilot arrangements require country-team agreement; see [../../planning/open-questions.md](../../planning/open-questions.md).

The **615** figure is the working DRC school-register count, not an independently confirmed final active-school total. No student records or sensitive personal information are included here.
