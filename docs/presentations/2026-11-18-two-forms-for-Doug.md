# Doug presentation: two final Kobo forms (18 November 2026)

**Suggested duration:** 6 minutes, then a short staging-Kobo demonstration.  
**Editable PowerPoint:** `SAWSO_Two_Final_Kobo_Forms_For_Doug_18Nov2026.pptx` (available in the project handover package; keep any actual XLSForms in approved storage rather than committing a school register to public GitHub).

## Slide 1 — Two forms. One school.
**Say:** "Today I'm showing you two forms, not two copies of one questionnaire. They serve different needs, but the same school can now be identified consistently in both."

## Slide 2 — Which is which?
**Assessment:** "How is the school doing, and what support does it need?" This comes from the February 2024 tool.  
**Conditions:** "Are governance, protection and mission-related institutional arrangements in place?" This is the VF questionnaire, not a second building assessment.

## Slide 3 — School Assessment: eight criteria
"Rather than one long descriptive answer for all the furniture, an assessor can record a type, quantity and condition in repeat entries. Core counts and evidence feed eight required provisional ratings, which in turn provide a draft weighted index and up to three support priorities."

**Qualify:** The formula can range from **25 to 100** and does not constitute an approved 0–100% measure. SAWSO must decide how rating anchors and fee-free schools should be treated.

## Slide 4 — Conditions Questionnaire: 14 VF conditions
"Sections A, B and C cover Governance & Compliance (4), Monitoring & Protection (5) and Mission & Faith Integration (5). We separated legal documents for land from legal permission for the school to operate, and removed repeated building/inventory questions that were already captured in the Assessment."

**Qualify:** Completed answers are **not** a compliance certification. Child-protection content should refer to institutional systems, not identifiable cases or children.

## Slide 5 — One school record, linked forms
"Both forms have the same 615-school selection list with unique `school_key` identifiers and the same assessor/school-header fields. This makes a future joined view plausible without asking staff to memorize codes."

**Be candid:** The 615-choice list is not yet an *approved active-school denominator*; **all** `managing_office` fields are currently `not_mapped`. The four-role dashboard is a **static illustrative prototype**, not connected to Kobo submissions or Power BI.

## Slide 6 — What Doug / DRC needs to decide
Ask for four decisions: (1) assign schools to managing offices, (2) sign off scoring—including C3/C7 and fee-free treatment, (3) approve VF evidence/safeguarding review roles, and (4) choose a small country-led field pilot.

**Closing line:** "The ideas are now visible in two consistent forms and a four-role concept. The next stage needs territorial leadership, schools and local developers to determine what should actually be implemented."

## Quick live demonstration: 90 seconds

Use **fictional data in staging Kobo projects**, not real staff/learner records.

1. Open the School Assessment: select a province, level and school; show that the key and IDs populate.
2. Show one infrastructure condition, one equipment repeat and the priority-support field.
3. Open the School Conditions form: select **the same school**, then demonstrate A1 land versus operating document and a B or C institutional condition.
4. Return to the four-role dashboard prototype and say explicitly that the dashboard is illustrative.
