## 2. One-page three-tier DRC data view

**Baseline** → collect stable school facts once and update when they change.

**Existing annual / termly** → preserve current DRC reporting obligations rather than inventing a new cycle.

**Proposed monthly** → keep to a short set of change signals:
1. student attendance
2. enrolment movement
3. teacher presence / class coverage
4. serious facility or WASH problem
5. priority support / disruption

## 3. Live Kobo preview

Demonstrate only the flow:
1. select province
2. select school from the master list
3. show school identifiers
4. open two or three assessment sections
5. show evidence + provisional score
6. show priority follow-up

Frame it as a **starter form for discussion**, not the final DRC assessment.

Do not spend the meeting troubleshooting Kobo Formbuilder, final weights, or unresolved DRC dropdown definitions.
